there is 3 types of users

patients, Doctors, admins

patients can post reviews on doctors pages
patients also comment on other reviews (reddit style)
patient can post that can be seen only from other clinics or doctors (Treatment Plan Request)
patients can get treatment plan and cost inquires from clinics and doctors by payment

doctors can respond the reviews,
doctors can edit their page (not the reviews from patients) and post links and images
doctors can claim to connected to clinics

admins can moderate conservations


Doctors can request to moderate clinic page from admins
Clinic pages can claim to have doctors
Clinic pages states location and communication info of the clinic

All type of users can be logged via google account.

Visuals
Ui is Dark, clean, modern,


Tech stack:
User login: Firebase auth, only google account login
Storage of the Data (Reviews, Doctor data): Firestore Database
Storage of the Images and Videos: Firebase Storage
